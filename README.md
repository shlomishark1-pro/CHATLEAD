# ChatLead 🚀

AI powered lead qualification and conversation analysis platform for businesses.

## Overview

ChatLead is an AI powered system designed to help businesses manage qualify and analyze incoming leads.

The platform analyzes customer conversations, extracts relevant information, evaluates lead quality, and provides businesses with a structured dashboard for managing their sales pipeline.

## Key Features

AI-powered lead qualification

Automated conversation analysis

Lead management dashboard

AI-generated insights from customer conversations

Identification of leads that may require attention

Sales pipeline tracking

Lead monitoring and alerts

AI assistant for lead and conversation insights

Subscription and billing infrastructure

AI monitoring and reliability mechanisms

AI & Automation

ChatLead uses AI to transform unstructured customer conversations into actionable business information.

The system is designed to
Extract relevant information from conversations

Identify customer intent

Evaluate lead quality

Summarize conversations

Help businesses prioritize leads

Provide structured insights through the dashboard
